# The council sees the world from within

The daily and session councils now share a small observation boundary: day,
food, wood, active conflicts and frontier warnings. Unknown supplies stay unknown.
The evidence is recorded beside each new decision and shown during observation.
External identity, chat, development metadata and event explanations do not enter
agenda selection. Council member identity still comes from the existing civic cast.

This is the first bounded subsystem, not a claim that every narrative system has
been converted. Next: give citizens local observations and persistent hypotheses,
and convert the remaining observer-aware dialogue without rewriting old history.

Validation: `node --test scripts/council-perception.test.js` exercises metadata
independence, real world changes and a complete browser council cycle.
